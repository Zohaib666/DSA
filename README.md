## Approach

For every important problem, I aim to understand:

1. What is the problem?
2. What is the brute-force approach?
3. Can it be optimized?
4. Why does the optimized approach work?
5. What is the time complexity?
6. What is the space complexity?
7. Can I solve a variation of the problem?
